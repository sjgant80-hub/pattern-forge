A pattern survives only if it generalises

Give the forge a domain's cases. It proposes simple patterns from a training split, then keeps only the ones that still predict a held-out split — graded by balanced accuracy, never an LLM judge. Overfit patterns die on data they never saw. Re-derived live in your browser from the gated kernel.
